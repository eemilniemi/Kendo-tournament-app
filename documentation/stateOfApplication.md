The quality of the Kendo App has been thoroughly evaluated through a comprehensive testing process, which included exploratory testing, unit testing, end-to-end (E2E) testing. Test tournaments were cancelled due to external factors. Here are the key conclusions regarding the product's quality:

1. Unit Testing:
The backend functionality was rigorously tested with 93 unit tests, all of which passed. This indicates a high level of reliability and correctness in the core backend components.

2. End-to-End Testing:
E2E tests were conducted to ensure the integration and functionality of both the frontend and backend. Most E2E tests passed, demonstrating that the application works mostly as intended from a user's perspective.

3. Test Tournaments:
Despite the failure of both scheduled test tournaments due to external factors (webserver update issues and a stolen database), the testing process provided valuable insights. These failures highlighted the importance of robust security measures and effective communication with stakeholders.

4. Bug Reporting and Resolution:
Numerous bugs were identified and resolved throughout the project. The bug reporting process ensured that issues were documented in Trello, tracked, and addressed efficiently. However, some bugs remain unresolved and are documented for future reference.

5. Customer Feedback:
Although the test tournaments did not yield direct feedback from end users, the new features of the app were successfully demoed to the customer. The customer has been able to test the new version of the Kendo App, which is now running at app.kendoliiga.fi.

6. Overall Quality:
KendoApp has demonstrated sufficient level of quality through its successful unit and E2E testing, though there are some important aspects to improve: security measures, modifying and deleting points on GameInterface, and password recovery.

In conclusion, the rigorous testing process, involving manual and automatic tests, has identified areas for improvement, particularly in security and GameInterface usability, which hopefully will be addressed in future iterations. The app is well-positioned to meet the needs of its users and stakeholders.
